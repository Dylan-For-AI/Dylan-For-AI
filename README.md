## ⚡ ABOUT ME

I'm Dylan, an **AI Application & Agent Engineer** focused on building intelligent, secure and autonomous systems.

My engineering philosophy is simple:

> **Don't just build AI applications. Engineer systems that can reason, act, protect and operate reliably.**

I work at the intersection of three demanding engineering domains:

- 🧠 **Autonomous AI Systems** — Multi-agent architectures, intelligent orchestration, tool calling and autonomous workflows.
- 🛡️ **AI Security & Safety** — Prompt injection defense, LLM guardrails, adversarial robustness and secure AI pipelines.
- 🏥 **HealthTech Engineering** — Secure medical RAG, sensitive data protection and architectures designed with GDPR and HDS requirements in mind.

I prioritize implementation over abstraction.

I build with Python, orchestrate agents, design retrieval pipelines, implement security layers and turn complex AI concepts into functional engineering systems.

**My objective:** Develop AI infrastructure capable of operating in environments where reliability, security and precision are non-negotiable.
